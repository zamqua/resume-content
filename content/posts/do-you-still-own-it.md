As a software system grows, it becomes difficult for humans to visualize or hold the whole system in their mind. The interaction of different components will become overwhelming.
AI tools can be a great help but leaving everything (designing, coding, and testing) to them will make the system more incomprehensible to humans.
I don't believe reading and reviewing the code written by AI will make us understand the system.
A fine balance is needed, where humans still understand without laboring in the creation of the system.
We have to dirty our hands, we have to challenge AI on why it chose certain data structure or design pattern. We need to write and/or edit the documentation. AI is not reducing our labor but rather moving it to a more abstract level of comprehending the system without writing the code.
